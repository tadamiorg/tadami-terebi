# Tadami Terebi (タダ見テレビ) v2.0.0

Tadami Terebi is the Android TV **Cast Connect receiver** companion to
[Tadami](https://github.com/tadamiorg/tadami). It runs on Android TV / Google TV and plays the
streams cast from the Tadami phone app, mirroring its player on the big screen: video-source and
subtitle selection, resume position, and episode switching all driven from the phone.

Like Tadami itself, it is a learning prototype, built to explore Android TV development with Kotlin,
Compose for TV, Media3/ExoPlayer and the Google Cast Connect SDK, and is not a production-ready app.

## Requirements

- An Android TV / Google TV device
- The [Tadami](https://github.com/tadamiorg/tadami) phone app, which acts as the Cast sender
